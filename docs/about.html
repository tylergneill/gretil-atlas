About this Atlas

A browsable index of the Sanskrit collection of GRETIL, the Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages (2001–2020), built from a scrape of the site shortly before its closure and archiving to TextGrid. This Atlas hosts no text; every work links to the files in the author's mirror.

What a work is here

GRETIL kept its Sanskrit texts in two layers: a categorical tree of legacy HTML files, and a curated TEI layer converted from about half of them between 2017 and 2020. This Atlas counts one work per TEI file, and one per legacy text that was never converted, with a text's several legacy renderings (analytic, plain, index) folded into one. Works are placed in GRETIL's own categories, taken from the directory their legacy file sat in. The choice of unit is provisional; the alternative under study is the site's own main-page entry.

Sizes

GRETIL is IAST throughout, so the byte figure is simply the body of each text with GRETIL's header, banner and character table removed. A TEI work is measured from its plaintext transformation; a legacy-only work from its largest rendering. Where a text exists in both layers the TEI body runs about a tenth larger than the legacy one, from structure labels and verse ids.

Growth

GRETIL published a dated update history, 498 entries from 2001 to 2020, each naming the texts it added. The growth series here is measured from that record: months, to . works could not be matched to an update, the Mahābhārata's parvans among them, and are left out of the series rather than dated by guess.

Caveats

  • Three official versions of GRETIL exist and disagree in detail; this Atlas follows the live site's last structure, which fixed thirteen misfilings the downloadable zip still carries.
  • The TextGrid archive holds some sixty TEI files more than the scrape; they are not here yet.
  • Pali, Prakrit, Dravidian and the other sections are inventoried but not counted.
